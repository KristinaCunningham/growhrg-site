HRG is a medical billing and revenue cycle company headquartered in Overland Park, Kansas, working with practices around the country since 1999 with staff based entirely in the United States. We own and operate this domain ourselves, so anyone who hears from us can check who we are before replying.

Case studies, specialty pages, leadership bios and the full list of services live on the main company website at healthcarerevenuegroup.com.

What our billing team does

The work covers the revenue cycle end to end. Claims are scrubbed before they go out so that fewer come back denied, and when one does come back we work the appeal rather than writing it off. Accounts receivable follow-up and recovery runs on a schedule instead of whenever someone finds time, and the billers assigned to an account run its weekly and monthly A/R reviews themselves.

Those same billers are who you talk to. Nobody relays your question to a person you never meet, and there is no offshore team.

Our specialists work inside the practice's own system and payer portals, which means an administrator sees the same claim, the same note and the same status that we do. Invoices come with a few days to review them before the money is debited.

We bill for clients on a set of supported EHR and practice management systems, and a short call confirms whether a practice's system is one of them.

Who we bill for

Practices from 2 to more than 100 providers in billing-complex specialties, including cardiology, dermatology, podiatry, rheumatology, urgent care, behavioral health, chiropractic, primary care, pain management and ENT, along with federally qualified health centers, multi-state telehealth groups and locum tenens agencies.

Most practices that call us have a story behind it. A billing vendor underperformed and nobody caught it for two quarters, or an in-house biller left in the middle of a cycle, or A/R climbed month after month while everyone was too busy seeing patients to chase it.

Credentialing and payer contracting are a separate practice at HRG with its own team and its own specialists. About a third of our billing clients started there. Details are on the main website.

What we do not do

  • We do not perform coding. We provide coding consultation as needed, and the coders sit on the client side.
  • We do not manage prior authorizations. We advise on workflow, and the process stays with the practice.
  • We do not use offshore teams.
  • We do not work in a separate dashboard. We work in the system the practice already uses.
  • We do not guarantee outcomes that belong to the payer, including payer decisions.

If you received an email from us

Mail from an @growhrg.com address comes from someone on HRG's billing side writing to a leader at a medical practice about billing or revenue cycle work. You did not subscribe to anything, and we will not add you to a newsletter. Every message carries a real person's name, a working reply address, and the phone number and postal address below.

To stop hearing from us, reply "no thanks" to any message and we will close your file. Every message also carries a one-click unsubscribe in the email header, which most mail apps show as an unsubscribe link near the sender's name. Either one works.
